# MyGlucoGuide Content Audit

Audit date: 2026-10-21
Branch: `main`
Scope: patient-facing copy and clinical-safety wording. No custom domain is configured yet.

## Changes made

- Home page glucose card now labels the displayed 112 mg/dL as an illustrative demo value, not a real patient reading or personal clinical interpretation.
- Home page food and fasting copy is more neutral and encourages discussion with a qualified clinician rather than implying individualized fasting guidance.
- CGM FAQ now explains that availability and smartphone compatibility vary by product, model, app, and supplier; readers are directed to current manufacturer compatibility information.
- CGM sensor wear duration is described as product-specific, with readers directed to the current instructions for use.
- CGM-versus-meter FAQ no longer presents a fixed 5–10 minute lag as universal and gives device-specific safety guidance.
- Diet FAQ now avoids implying a single meal sequence is right for everyone and recommends sustainable choices with professional guidance.
- CGM trend-arrow descriptions no longer assign universal numerical rates to arrows; interpretation is described as device-specific.
- CGM safety copy now directs readers to manufacturer instructions and their clinician when sensor readings and symptoms do not match.

## Remaining content checks

1. Review every article reference for currentness and ensure each reference supports the specific claim next to it. Several article entries cite 2024 guidance; verify against the latest applicable guidance before presenting as current.
2. Review all CGM comparison claims against the exact product labeling and avoid treating features as universal across brands.
3. Verify operational statements such as supplier/authorized availability, response hours, and support-team descriptions before publishing them as facts.
4. Test all contact and WhatsApp paths with real environment configuration before launch.
5. Confirm the final production URL before adding canonical URLs, absolute social image URLs, or a sitemap that names the production domain.
6. Once hosted, validate page metadata and structured data, generate/verify the XML sitemap, submit it in Google Search Console, and check indexing.

## Safety and trust principles

- Educational content must not diagnose, prescribe, or replace clinician advice.
- Device capabilities, wear duration, alerts, compatibility, and instructions are product-specific.
- Demo readings and example charts must be visibly labeled as illustrative rather than patient data.
- Do not claim that SEO, build, accessibility, or live deployment checks have passed until they are actually run.
